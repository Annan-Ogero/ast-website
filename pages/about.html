Our Story

Built on Craft, Curiosity, and Code

Annan Software Team began as a group of determined developers who shared one belief: software built in Africa should be just as excellent as anything built anywhere else in the world. Led by Annan Ogero — a frontend developer, digital entrepreneur, and educator — AST grew from a personal project into a complete full-stack team.

Today, AST delivers web applications, mobile apps, 3D experiences, cybersecurity audits, and email design solutions to clients across Kenya and beyond. We also teach software development at St. Paul's Victorious Training College and through our WhatsApp online classroom.

Education

We Don't Just Build — We Teach

Part of what makes AST unique is that several of our team members also teach software development at St. Paul's Victorious Training College — a Nairobi institution where we've helped shape the next generation of Kenyan developers.

We also run an active WhatsApp learning community with hundreds of members who receive structured lessons, code reviews, and mentorship online — making quality software education accessible beyond physical classrooms.
